## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Section jumps
`]` SHALL jump to the start of the next section and `[` to the start of the current section, or the previous one when pressed within the first bar of a section. The keys SHALL work in the player window.

#### Scenario: Next section
- **WHEN** the user presses ] during the second section of an analyzed track
- **THEN** playback continues from the start of the third section
