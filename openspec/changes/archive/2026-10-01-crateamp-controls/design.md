## Context

- **Audio:** the renderer runs the 10-band EQ, then the tap, then volume, in the real-time callback (no allocation, locks or syscalls; `rt_alloc` enforces it). Parameters cross threads through atomics. The renderer is reconfigured when the sample rate changes.
- **EQ section:** ON at x 14–40 and the response graph from x 86 on the top row (y 18), PRESETS at x 217. There is no AUTO button, so x 44–82 of that row is free.
- **Playlist:**
  - BPM is on `Entry`, folded into 88–176 and known only once a track has been analysed.
  - The play order comes from `Playlist::play_order`, and the engine queue is rebuilt when it changes.
  - The preview horizon (what downloads ahead) and preparation follow the same play order.
  - Rows are drawn by crate index, and the drag-to-reorder and Move actions use indices.
  - The footer's `pl_info` is a 100 px time readout ("sel/total"), with no room for more text. The title bar shows the crate's name, centred.
- **Crates:** chosen from the title-bar menu, which marks the shown crate with • and the playing one with ⏵. Send to crate skips entries the crate already holds and says how many were sent.
- **Skin:** generated by `skin-gen` from `skin/generate.rs`. A test checks that the committed atlas matches, so new sprites mean regenerating it.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A musical filter sweep, click-free.
- Narrow a crate to a tempo range in one move, and have playback follow it.
- See and use crates without opening a menu.

**Non-Goals:**
- Styles on entries, style or genre chips (dropped).
- A footer sort icon (dropped: OPT ▸ Sort and the column headers already sort).
- The mockup's "BPM Lock" and "VINYL RIP" (dropped).
- Filters across crates (a search).
- The new look from `crateamp-look`: this change uses the current skin and doesn't wait for it.

## Decisions

**1. The low-pass filter.**
- A 2-pole resonant low-pass biquad (RBJ cookbook) on the stereo output, after the EQ and before the tap, so the visuals and the spectrum see the sweep too.
- Q ≈ 1.2: a slight peak, musical, no self-oscillation.
- The knob maps 0..1 logarithmically to 60 Hz..20 kHz. At 1.0 the filter is **bypassed exactly** (not a filter at 20 kHz), so "off" is bit-transparent.
- The target cutoff is an atomic `f32`. The callback moves toward it with a one-pole smoother of about 20 ms, and recomputes coefficients every 32 samples, so there's no zipper noise and no allocation. Coefficients are recomputed when the sample rate changes.
- Leaving bypass and returning to it crossfade over the smoother's time, so switching on or off doesn't click.
- The knob is not remembered: every launch starts with it off.
- It sits in the EQ's top row at x 46, 14 × 14 skin px, labelled LP, between ON and the graph. It's dragged up and down, and double-clicked to reset. Its tooltip shows the cutoff ("LP 2.4 kHz", or "LP off").

**2. The filter bar.**
- One row above the list (and above the column header, when there is one). It's shown when the shown crate has at least two different known tempos; otherwise a range means nothing.
- Left: **ALL**. Then the BPM slider, with two handles snapping to whole BPM between the crate's lowest and highest known tempo, and the range as text ("124-140 BPM", the skin's font having no en dash).
- Matching: an entry passes when its BPM is inside the range. Entries without a BPM pass only while the range is the full span (the filter is off).
- Per crate, `(min, max)` is saved with the crate. It's widened back to the full span when the crate's tempos no longer reach it, and dropped when it covers the full span.
- While the filter is on, the title bar reads "‹NAME› · 42/301" (matching / all). It's shortened like the name, keeping the count.
- When nothing matches, the list says "No tracks match" with an ALL button.

**3. What shows and plays under the filter.**
- `Playlist::play_order` takes the filter. Next, previous, shuffle and the pre-warm use only matching entries.
- The playing entry keeps playing when hidden, and the next one is the next matching entry after it in crate order.
- Changing the filter rebuilds the engine queue, the same path as a sort or reorder.
- The rows list only matching entries, but **numbers stay crate positions** ("12." is still entry 12).
- A row maps to its entry, not its index. Drag-to-reorder moves the dragged entry to the crate position of the entry under the pointer, as without a filter. Keyboard navigation, the cursor and Select all / Invert work over the shown entries.
- **P** with a hidden playing entry turns the filter off first, then shows the entry as now.
- Things that act on the crate, not the view:
  - sort (filtered rows stay filtered);
  - export M3U (the whole crate);
  - Remove album, whose label counts the whole album.
- **Previews:** the horizon follows the filtered play order, so downloads serve what will actually play.
  - The trade-off: under a range, entries without a BPM are hidden. They are learned only when they're played or prepared, or from the score cache.
  - The filter bar says so as "+n without BPM" after the range text when there are any.

**4. The crate sidebar.**
- It's 110 skin px wide, inside the playlist, left of the list. It shows when the playlist is at least 600 skin px wide or maximized, and the sidebar is on.
- A ☰ button in the title bar at x 238 (left of ⇔) toggles it, on by default and remembered in settings. Narrower than 600 px, the button still shows, dimmed, and does nothing.
- Rows: the name, cut to fit; the count right-aligned; ⏵ for the playing crate, • for the shown one (the crate menu's marks). An unreadable crate is dimmed and can't be chosen.
- Click: show. Right-click: Rename…, Delete… (with the usual confirmation for a crate with entries). "+ New crate" at the bottom opens the same name dialog as the menu.
- **Dropping entries:** while dragging entries (the reorder drag), a crate row under the pointer highlights, and dropping there sends the dragged entries exactly as Send to crate does. That means the selection if the dragged entry is in it, the same skipping of duplicates and the same message. Dropping on the shown crate does nothing. Dropping inside the list still reorders.

**5. Skin.** `generate.rs` gains the knob's face (the app draws its pointer at the knob's angle, green while the filter is on) and the "LP" label, the ☰ button (normal and pressed), and the slider's track and handle. `skin-gen` regenerates the atlas, and its test stays green.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The filter in the real-time path] → no allocation (test), coefficients updated every 32 samples, smoothing, an exact bypass when off. `--click-test` and `rt_alloc` must stay green.
- [A BPM range hides untested tracks in a fresh dig] → "+n without BPM" in the bar, and ALL is one click. Downloading hidden previews just to learn their tempo would break "nothing is fetched for a BPM".
- [Hidden entries confusing the user] → the count in the title bar, crate numbers kept on rows.
- [Rows no longer equal to indices] → the row-to-entry mapping is built once per frame from the filter; every row action uses entry ids.
- [The sidebar takes list space] → only from 600 px, and it can be hidden.
